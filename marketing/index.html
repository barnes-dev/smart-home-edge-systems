Smart Home Edge Systems / Community support

Help build the home lab.

This project is building an affordable, private, in-home testbed for local AI, voice assistants, automation, media, storage, and resilient household infrastructure. Support makes it possible to test real products and publish the results for everyone.

What support unlocks

Hardware and software become documented, repeatable solutions instead of untested recommendations. Every contribution helps turn a working experiment into a resource another household can use.

01 / Why support matters

A personal project with a public purpose.

I am building this lab from a fixed income, one tested system at a time.

I am a United States Air Force veteran and retired from LM Human Space due to a disability. My income is fixed, but my commitment to this work is full-time. I am using the engineering discipline, technical experience, and practical focus I have developed over decades to build a community resource around private, local-first technology.

The lab is not intended to sell vague promises. It is intended to show what works in an ordinary home: which hardware is sufficient, which software integrates cleanly, what local AI costs to operate, where latency appears, and how a household can keep control of its own data.

02 / Current needs

Support can be practical.

New or used equipment is welcome when it can contribute to a documented test, comparison, or deployment guide.

01 / HARDWARE

Compute and networking

Mini PCs, GPUs, memory, storage, switches, access points, microphones, voice satellites, and networking equipment for local AI and infrastructure testing.

02 / SOFTWARE

Licenses and access

Software licenses, developer access, hosted services for comparison, test accounts, and tools that improve measurement, documentation, or accessibility.

03 / OPERATIONS

Lab expenses

Replacement parts, power protection, cabling, media production, data storage, test fixtures, and the recurring costs of maintaining a reliable testbed.

Supporters will be acknowledged in a clear, useful way that connects the contribution to the work it enabled.
